Is the Samsung Galaxy A06 Still Worth Buying in 2026? An Honest Buyer Take

Editorial October 1, 2026 Opinion

The Samsung Galaxy A06 is the phone that refuses to leave the shelf. It was never exciting, never the best value, and never the one reviewers recommended first. Yet it remains one of the most visible budget Samsungs in Kenyan shops, often priced lower than its newer siblings. In 2026, that low price is the only reason anyone still asks about it.

The honest answer is uncomfortable. The A06 is no longer worth buying for most people. Not because it is broken, but because the market has moved past it while Samsung has quietly stopped supporting it.

The Price Is the Only Argument Left

Local pricing for the Galaxy A06 is scattered. Jumia Kenya lists a 4GB/128GB unit at KES 17,439 , while another listing shows KES 18,140 . Those are not budget prices. They are prices for a phone that should cost less. A March 2026 guide recorded the 4GB/64GB variant at KES 10,107 , and one Kenyan publication claimed a KES 11,900 figure that it also incorrectly described as 5G-capable . The A06 is a 4G phone. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

If you can find the 64GB model near KES 10,000, the price becomes defensible for basic use. At KES 17,000 or above, it is poor value. The Redmi 15C and Infinix Hot 60i offer more memory, larger batteries, and faster charging for less money .

What You Get for the Money

The spec sheet is entry-level in every sense. The display is a 6.7-inch PLS LCD at 720p resolution with a 60Hz refresh rate . That is a soft, slow panel by 2026 standards. The processor is a MediaTek Helio G85, a chip that handles WhatsApp and M-Pesa but struggles with anything heavier . The battery is 5,000mAh with 25W charging, and the main camera is 50MP .

The one genuine strength is storage expansion. The A06 has a dedicated microSD slot supporting cards up to 1TB, alongside dual SIM support . For a student carrying offline lecture notes or a trader storing receipts, that flexibility matters. Samsung's Knox Vault security is also present, which is more than most budget rivals offer .

But those strengths do not outweigh the core problem.

The Software Promise Is Broken

This is the fact that changes everything. The Galaxy A06 4G lost Android updates in August 2026 . The phone will continue receiving security patches for a period, but the major Android version upgrades are finished. If you buy an A06 today, you are buying a device that will not receive Android 17, Android 18, or anything beyond what it already has.

For years, the standard advice in Kenya was to buy Samsung because of its update commitment. That advice was correct for the Galaxy A07, which is promised six major upgrades. It does not apply to the A06. The A06 is the phone Samsung abandoned first.

User reviews reflect the decline. Samsung's own community pages are filled with complaints about lag, freezing, and slow performance, particularly after software updates . One user described it as "very very slow" and "bad for programming" . Another reported the phone dying shortly after the warranty period . These are not isolated complaints. They are a pattern.

How It Compares to What You Should Buy Instead

At the same or lower price, the Xiaomi Redmi 15C offers a 6,000mAh battery, a 120Hz display, and 128GB storage . It is not perfect. The screen is still 720p. But it is newer, faster, and supported.

The Infinix Hot 60i offers NFC for M-Pesa Tap-to-Pay, a feature the A06 lacks entirely . For an urban Kenyan buyer, that is a practical advantage that will only grow as Pesapal and Safaricom expand contactless payments.

Even within Samsung's own lineup, the Galaxy A07 is the better buy. It costs more, but it comes with a six-year update promise and a newer processor. Paying extra for the A07 is not spending more. It is avoiding the mistake of buying a phone that is already at the end of its supported life.

The Verdict

Buy the Samsung Galaxy A06 if: you find the 64GB variant at or below KES 10,000, you need a Samsung-branded phone for Knox security or service network reasons, and you accept that it will not receive future Android updates. It is a device for calls, WhatsApp, and M-Pesa only.

Skip it if: you can spend anything more than KES 10,000. The Redmi 15C, Infinix Hot 60i, and Galaxy A07 are all better choices at or near this price. The A06 is not a budget phone anymore. It is an old phone sold at a budget price, and those are not the same thing.

The Galaxy A06 was a reasonable entry-level phone in 2024. In 2026, it is a phone you buy only when the price is too low to ignore and your expectations are equally low. For everyone else, the money is better spent elsewhere.
