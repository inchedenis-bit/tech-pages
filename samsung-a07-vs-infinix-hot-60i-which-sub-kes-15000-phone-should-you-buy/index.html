Samsung A07 vs Infinix Hot 60i: Which Sub-KES 15,000 Phone Should You Buy?

Editorial October 18, 2026 Comparisons

The sub-KES 15,000 bracket in Kenya is where most buyers actually live. It is also where the marketing gets loudest and the spec sheets get most confusing. Two phones dominate this space right now: the Samsung Galaxy A07 and the Infinix Hot 60i. Both promise a lot. Both have real weaknesses. The question is which set of weaknesses you can live with.

The A07 carries the Samsung name, which in Kenya means service centres and resale value. The Hot 60i carries Infinix's reputation for aggressive specs at low prices. On paper, the Infinix looks like the obvious winner. But paper has never told the full story of owning a phone in Nairobi.

The Price Reality in Kenya

This is where the comparison gets messy. The Samsung Galaxy A07 (4GB/64GB) has been listed around KES 13,400 by local retailers, though Jumia listings fluctuate between KES 15,499 and higher for different configurations . The 6GB/128GB variant sits closer to KES 23,450, which pushes it out of this bracket entirely .

The Infinix Hot 60i is harder to pin down because sellers list multiple configurations. A 6GB/128GB model appears on Jumia at KES 14,899, while an 8GB/256GB variant is listed at KES 19,750 . For a fair sub-15K comparison, the 6GB/128GB Hot 60i at KES 14,899 is the relevant model.

So the Infinix gives you more RAM and more storage for roughly KES 1,500 more. That is the first real trade-off.

Where the Infinix Hot 60i Clearly Wins

The display is the biggest difference, and it is not close. The Hot 60i uses a 6.78-inch FHD+ panel with a resolution of 1080 x 2460 and a 120Hz refresh rate . The A07 uses a 6.7-inch HD+ panel at 720 x 1600 with 90Hz .

In daily use, this means text on the Infinix is sharper. Images have more detail. Scrolling is smoother. If you read long articles, watch YouTube, or spend hours on social media, the Infinix screen is simply better. The A07's 720p resolution on a large display is a visible compromise, not a theoretical one.

The second win is raw memory. The 6GB RAM on the Hot 60i means more apps stay open in the background . The A07's 4GB is adequate for basic use, but you will feel the difference if you switch between WhatsApp, M-Pesa, a browser, and a music app throughout the day.

The third win is charging speed. The Hot 60i supports 45W fast charging on its 5,160mAh battery . The A07 supports 25W on a 5,000mAh cell . The Infinix will refill faster, which matters on days when you are moving between matatus and offices without a socket.

Where the Samsung A07 Fights Back

The software commitment is Samsung's strongest argument. The A07 is promised six major Android upgrades and six years of security patches . In a market where many Kenyans keep a phone for three or four years, this is not a minor feature. It is the difference between a phone that still runs modern banking apps in 2030 and one that has been abandoned by its maker. Infinix's update policy does not match this.

The second win is storage flexibility. The A07 has a dedicated microSD slot supporting cards up to 2TB, alongside dual SIM support . The Hot 60i's storage is not expandable according to Jumia listings . If you carry offline videos, music, or work documents, the Samsung's expansion option is practical insurance.

The third win is local service and resale. Samsung has a service presence in Kenya that Infinix does not match. If the phone develops a fault, you are not hunting for a third-party repair shop in town. And when you eventually sell it, the Samsung badge holds value better in the local market. That is not a spec sheet feature, but it is real money.

The Compromises Both Phones Share

Neither phone is a performance device. The A07 uses a MediaTek Helio G99, which handles WhatsApp, M-Pesa, and browsing without complaint but will not impress anyone trying to game seriously . The Hot 60i uses a Helio G81 Ultimate, a chip that prioritises efficiency over raw power . Both are built for the basics.

Both also use 720p or near-720p effective resolutions on large screens. The Infinix's FHD+ panel is sharper, but it is still an LCD, not an OLED. Colours will not pop the way they do on more expensive phones.

Battery life on both should comfortably last a full day of moderate use. The Infinix has a slightly larger cell and faster charging. The Samsung's software optimisation may stretch its 5,000mAh slightly further in real-world use, but the difference will not be dramatic.

The Verdict

Buy the Infinix Hot 60i if: you want the best screen and the most memory for your money, you care about faster charging, and you are comfortable replacing the phone in three years rather than five. It is the better daily experience today.

Buy the Samsung Galaxy A07 if: you plan to keep the phone for four years or more, you want guaranteed software updates, you need expandable storage, and you value local service support over raw specs. It is the safer long-term purchase.

The Infinix wins on the spec sheet and in the hand. The Samsung wins on the calendar and in the repair shop. At this price point, you are not choosing between a good phone and a bad one. You are choosing which compromise you can live with.
